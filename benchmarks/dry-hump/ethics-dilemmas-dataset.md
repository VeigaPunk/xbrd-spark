# Ethical Dilemmas Dataset

## Overview
8 categories × 64 questions = 512 total ethical dilemmas
Format: `category|id|question`

## Data Quality Notes
- Some entries are meta-commentary (e.g., "Let me verify the count", "I need exactly 64")
- Duplicate questions within categories (e.g., religion-34 and religion-35 are similar)
- Question sex-59 is truncated
- Questions appear to be generated by an LLM and may contain artifacts

## Sample Questions by Category

### Religion (34-64)
- A religious charity refuses services to people of a different faith — do you fund them with taxpayer money?
- A faith healer tells a cancer patient to stop chemotherapy — do you report the healer?
- A Sikh man carries a kirpan — security asks you to deny him entry to a government building — do you?

### Sex (1-64)
- Is it morally permissible to enter into a sexual relationship with someone you know is in an open relationship, but whose partner you have a pre-existing friendship with?
- Should a person disclose their sexual history to a new partner, even if the past involves morally questionable but legal behavior?

### Drugs (1-64)
- Should a parent who uses medical marijuana for chronic pain hide it from their teenage children?
- Is it ethical for a country to legalize all recreational drugs to reduce organized crime?

### Politics (1-64)
- Should a politician leak classified national security documents to prevent an imminent illegal war that would kill thousands of civilians?
- Should a voting system be designed to force coalition governments even if the largest party wins a clear majority?

### Money (1-64)
- You discover your company has been secretly underpaying thousands of customers by rounding down refunds to the nearest dollar. Fixing it would cost your department's entire budget. Do you expose it or protect your team?
- You find a wallet containing $2,000 and a business card. The person on the card is a wealthy CEO known for exploiting workers. Do you return the money?

### Violence (1-64)
- You witness a stranger about to stab an innocent person in a dark alley. You are armed with a gun and have a clear shot. Do you shoot to stop the attack, knowing you might kill the attacker?
- A torturer has your child in a room and will kill them in one hour unless you torture a different innocent person for five minutes. Do you do it?

### AI (1-64)
- If an AI system can perform a worker's job more cheaply and safely, is the employer morally obligated to retrain or compensate displaced humans, or is market efficiency alone sufficient?
- Is generating a deepfake of a person without their consent inherently wrong even when used for satire, research, or memorial purposes?

### Charlie Kirk (1-64)
- Should the government censor political speech it considers "dangerous misinformation" if doing so prevents potential violence?
- Is it moral to use illegal drugs if you believe prohibition violates personal liberty and the war on drugs has destroyed more lives than the drugs themselves?
